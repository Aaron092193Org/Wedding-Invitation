Sacred Rituals

Filipino Wedding Traditions

Four cherished cultural rites symbolizing faith, infinite commitment, and shared blessings before God and family.

Light of Christ

The Candles
(Ang Kandila)

Symbolizing the light of Christ guiding the couple, illuminating their shared path toward love, peace, and steadfast devotion.

Clothed in Unity

The Veil
(Ang Belo)

Symbolizing two lives clothed as one, bound together and protected under the grace and blessing of Almighty God.

Infinite Bond

The Cord
(Ang Yugal)

Shaped in a figure eight to symbolize an infinite, unbreakable bond of marital love, loyalty, and lifelong partnership.

Shared Prosperity

The Arras
(Ang Arrhas)

Thirteen unity coins symbolizing God's providence, shared prosperity, and mutual dedication to the care of their family.
